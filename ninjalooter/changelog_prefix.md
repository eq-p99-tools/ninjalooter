## Did you know?
* If you rename the executable to `ninjalooter.exe`, it will auto-replace itself on update, so your shortcuts will always point to the newest version.
* Bid messages are automatically copied whenever an auction changes state. You don't need to press the "Copy" button immediately after pressing `DKP Bid` or `Complete`. This actually applies to almost any button that could conceivably change paste text.
* Nearly every list item has a double-click action. Hover the area for pop-up tips regarding these actions and more!
* If you haven't launched in a while, check out some of the previous changelogs: https://github.com/rm-you/ninjalooter/releases
